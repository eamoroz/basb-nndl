5. Key Insight & Conclusion

Sex is the single most influential factor associated with passenger death.

  • Male passengers show the highest normalized death rate (~81%)
  • Female passengers show a substantially lower death rate (~26%)
  • This gap is the largest among all categorical features
  • Sex also exhibits the strongest correlation with Survived (≈ −0.54)

Based on consistent evidence from death-rate comparisons, numerical encoding, and correlation analysis, passenger sex dominates all other examined factors in association with mortality.
