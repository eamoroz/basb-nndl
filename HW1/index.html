5. Key Insight & Conclusion

Sex is the single most influential factor associated with passenger death.

  • Male death rate ≈ 81% vs female ≈ 26%
  • Largest categorical death-rate gap among all features
  • Strongest correlation magnitude with Survived

This dominance is consistently confirmed by death-rate bar charts, numerical comparisons, and Pearson correlation analysis.
