5. Key Insight

Across every analysis, Sex emerges as the dominant factor associated with passenger death.

  • Male death rate is dramatically higher than female.
  • The gap persists across passenger classes and embarkation ports.
  • Sex shows the strongest correlation with survival.
  • Effect size exceeds that of class, age, fare, or family size.

Conclusion: Being male was the single most important factor associated with death on the Titanic.
