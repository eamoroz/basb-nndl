5. Key Insight & Conclusion

Sex is the single most influential factor associated with passenger death.

  • Largest death-rate gap observed between males and females
  • Consistent dominance across categorical and numerical visualizations
  • Highest absolute Pearson correlation with survival outcome

Based on descriptive statistics, normalized death rates, multiple visual comparisons, and correlation magnitude, being male is most strongly associated with death aboard the Titanic.
