5. Key Insight & Conclusion

Sex is the single most influential factor associated with passenger death.

  • Largest death-rate gap between categories (male vs female)
  • Consistent dominance across categorical analysis
  • Strongest absolute correlation with Survived

Male passengers experienced a substantially higher probability of death than females, exceeding the effects observed for passenger class, embarkation port, and all numerical features.
