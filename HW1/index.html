5. Key Insight & Conclusion

Sex is the single most influential factor associated with passenger death.

  • Largest death-rate difference between categories
  • Clear separation in normalized bar charts
  • Highest absolute Pearson correlation with Survived

Male passengers experienced substantially higher mortality across all analyses, exceeding the effect size of class, age, fare, and embarkation port.
